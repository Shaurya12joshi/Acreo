How it works

Three steps. Zero copy-pasting.

1. Browse as usual

Search for rentals on MagicBricks, 99acres, or NoBroker exactly the way you normally would.

2. Acreo collects quietly

Every listing that appears in your results is saved to your own browser storage nothing to click, nothing sent anywhere.

3. Open the popup, compare

Click the Acreo icon to see everything you've come across in one sortable, filterable table.

Features

Built for comparing, not just collecting

Auto-capture across sites

Listings from MagicBricks, 99acres, and NoBroker all land in the same table, same format no manual entry.

Smart filters

Narrow by source, BHK count, furnishing status, or preferred tenant type click a chip, done.

On-demand detail loading

Missing floor, bathrooms, or gated security info? Hit “Load details” and Acreo fetches it from the listing page.

Private by default

Everything lives in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, ever.

One click back to source

Every row keeps a direct “View” link back to the original listing when you're ready to reach out.

Clear all, anytime

Starting a fresh search? One click empties your list so old listings don't clutter new comparisons.

Filters

Your list, your browser, nobody else.

Acreo stores everything with chrome.storage.local. There's no backend, no account, and no listing data ever leaves your device “Load details” only ever talks to the listing page you already clicked on.

Install

Get set up in under a minute

1. Download the extension folder

Grab the Acreo files and unzip them somewhere you won't move, like a “Chrome Extensions” folder.

2. Open chrome://extensions

Paste that into your address bar, or go to Chrome's menu → Extensions → Manage Extensions.

3. Turn on Developer mode

Flip the switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.

4. Click “Load unpacked”

Select the Acreo folder you downloaded. The icon appears in your toolbar immediately.

5. Start browsing rentals

Head to MagicBricks, 99acres, or NoBroker and search as usual Acreo takes it from there.
